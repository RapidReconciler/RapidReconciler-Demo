Complex Passwords

Choose which companies require stronger passwords for password sign-ins. Because one person can work in more than one company, the strongest rule wins — if someone can reach any company that requires a complex password, they’ll be asked for one. People who sign in with single sign-on (SSO) are never affected.

What each setting requires

Standard Always on

  • At least 8 characters.
  • Can’t reuse your current password.

Complex When a company requires it

  • Everything in Standard, plus:
  • Characters from at least 3 of 4 groups — uppercase, lowercase, digit, special.
  • No run of 3+ characters from your name or email.
  • Can’t match any of your last 10 passwords.
