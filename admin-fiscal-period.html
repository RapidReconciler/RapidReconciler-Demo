Period-End Date Correction

When a fiscal-calendar change moves a period-ending date, the reporting tables keep the old date until it’s corrected. This replaces the old date with the corrected one everywhere it’s stored, for all companies.

This is an administrator function. Contact your administrator to correct a period-ending date.
